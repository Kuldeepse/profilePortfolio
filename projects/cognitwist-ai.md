# Cognitwist AI — Career Intelligence Product Case Study

**Product:** Career discovery and role-alignment workflows. **Stage:** Ongoing product, data-quality and UX development.

## User problem
Candidates must search fragmented vacancies, assess relevance, tailor their applications and distinguish active jobs from outdated listings.

## Product journey
1. Specify role, location, seniority and working preferences.
2. Discover jobs from relevant sources and link to the original employer application.
3. Evaluate role relevance using structured criteria and transparent reasoning.
4. Review role details in a responsive list-and-details interface.
5. Produce tailored CV/application material and revisit saved opportunities.

## Design and technical decisions being explored
- Prefer canonical employer/ATS evidence over aggregator-only listings.
- Normalise titles, locations, salary and posting dates.
- Deduplicate postings and flag unverifiable availability.
- Separate retrieval evidence from model-generated relevance summaries.
- Provide filtering, responsive layouts and an accessible details panel.
- Consider LLM costs, governance, rate limits, evaluations and a trial-to-paid product model.

## Evaluation framework
Measure verified-listing rate, duplicate rate, source-link validity, search relevance, match explanation quality, mobile task success and time-to-application. These are **proposed product metrics**, not published measured results.

## Evidence boundary
The [career website](https://cognitwistai.duckdns.org/career) is a project destination, not independently verified current uptime or feature acceptance. Screenshots, benchmark results and actual production flows are pending publication.
